WallPanel Reborn is a free, open-source app maintained by beecho01. It is provided as is, at no cost.

## Information Collection and Use

WallPanel Reborn does not collect, transmit or sell any personal information. It contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.

Everything you enter in the app, such as dashboard URLs, MQTT broker details and passwords, is stored only on your device. The app sends data only to servers you configure yourself, such as your dashboard and your MQTT broker.

## Camera

If you enable camera features (motion detection, face detection, QR code reading or MJPEG streaming), images are processed on the device. They are only made available over the network through the MJPEG streaming server, and only if you turn it on.

## Third-Party Services

Face detection and QR code reading use Google Play Services on devices that have it. Google's [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies to Google Play Services.

## Links to Other Sites

The app shows web pages you choose. Those sites are not operated by us, and their own privacy policies apply.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes are posted on this page.

This policy is effective as of 2026-10-21.

## Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/beecho01/wallpanel-reborn/issues).
